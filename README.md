# Halo Bot 🤖

A desk-robot pet that lives in your Android phone. It sees you through the front camera, follows you with its eyes, makes faces, talks, asks questions, plays games, and **moves using the phone's own vibration motor** (the Cycloramic trick).

**Install:** open the [latest release](../../releases/latest) on your phone, download `HaloBot.apk`, and allow installing from your browser when Android asks. Every push to `main` builds a new release automatically.

## How to use it
- Prop the phone up in **landscape**, facing you. For vibration movement, put it on a smooth surface or a phone turntable. Flat-edged phones (like the S24 Ultra) can stand on their edge and spin.
- **Tap** = poke · **Double tap** = talk · **Swipe** = pet · **Long press** = settings

## What it can do
**Face & personality**
- 50+ procedural expressions (happy, joy, laugh, love, excited, sad, cry, angry, furious, grumpy, sleepy, bored, curious, confused, suspicious, shy, wink, smug, dizzy, scared, focused, thinking, proud, hungry, eating, cool shades, singing, party, spooky, kiss, embarrassed, determined...)
- Eyes that blink, double-blink, glance around, grow on the side they're looking toward, and breathe
- Hearts, stars, spirals, tears, sweat, zzz, music notes, anger marks, steam, sparkles, confetti, weather scenes
- 6 eye colors plus a retro scanline mode
- Moods (happiness, energy, boredom) that drift over time and change its behaviour
- Idle life: yawns, sneezes, hiccups, humming, hopping, getting bored and asking to play
- Falls asleep when nobody's around, at night, or in the dark. Optional dim night clock
- Holiday moods: Halloween, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's Day
- Birthday surprise: cake, song, and blow out the candles for real (it listens through the mic)

**Sees you** (on-device ML Kit, works offline)
- Follows your face with its eyes and mirrors your head tilt
- Greets you by name and time of day. Says "I missed you" after a long time away
- Smiles back when you smile, winks back when you wink, gets shy when you're too close
- Plays peekaboo, notices new people, gets excited when there are several faces
- Buzzes around looking for you when you leave

**Feels things**
- Pet it (purrs), poke it (giggles, but gets angry if you poke too much)
- Pick it up ("Wheee!"), shake it (dizzy), drop it ("Aaah!"), turn it face down (sleeps)
- Tilt it and its eyes slide; wave over the top sensor; turn off the lights
- Plug in the charger and it "eats". It asks for food when the battery is low
- Startles at loud noises and auto-dances when music plays

**Voice commands** (offline): time, date, clock, battery, weather, timers, alarms, reminders, focus/pomodoro, selfies with countdown, flashlight, music play/pause/next and volume, math, spelling, counting, coin flip, dice, random numbers, crystal ball, jokes, facts, stories, riddles, songs, compliments, 30 animal sounds, names, birthday, favorite things, eye color, quiet mode and more. Long-press for the full list.

**Games:** rock paper scissors, trivia (streaks), riddles, staring contest (it really checks if you blink), hide and seek, guess the number, guess my mood, plus a breathing exercise.

**AI brain (optional):** add a Claude API key in settings and it can chat about anything, with a facial expression to match each reply.
